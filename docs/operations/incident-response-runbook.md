# Incident Response Runbook

## Detect and Triage

Classify availability, confidentiality, integrity, authentication, database, backup, Job, import, AI, and storage symptoms. Preserve privacy-safe correlation identifiers, Security Events, Job Attempts, release identity, and bounded operational logs. Do not copy manuscript content into incident notes.

## Contain

Enable maintenance mode, stop workers, restrict operational access, revoke affected sessions, and isolate compromised service credentials. Do not retry ambiguous provider effects or resume restored Jobs. Preserve evidence before destructive repair.

## Rotate and Recover

Use the secret-rotation runbook for signing, database, provider, backup, encryption-key reference, and deployment credentials. Determine whether forward repair, application rollback, or isolated verified restoration is appropriate. Before restoration activation, invalidate sessions, quarantine unfinished Jobs/imports/AI, reset search projections, review Grants, and verify current Revision/version integrity.

## Return to Service

Run Django deploy checks, migration/readiness checks, synthetic critical workflows, backup monitoring, and worker readiness. Disable maintenance only after owner authority and recovery evidence are reviewed. Notify the owner when private-data exposure, credential compromise, integrity loss, or material unavailability is reasonably suspected.

## Review

Record timeline, release/configuration, containment, decisions, evidence custody, affected categories, recovery verification, and corrective actions. Rotate temporary break-glass credentials and revoke exceptional access. This process makes no legal or regulatory compliance claim.
