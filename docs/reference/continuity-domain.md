# Continuity domain

Continuity records preserve distinct narrative layers. Objective story truth belongs in canonical story and world records. A Plot Thread tracks a narrative promise and its development; a Secret records a hidden truth and the public belief around it. Clues are evidence planted for a Thread, while Reveals are author-planned disclosures or reversals. Thread Events provide a chronological progress log.

Reader Knowledge records what the audience has learned or been led to believe. Character Knowledge records one Character's knowledge state, certainty, source, and story provenance. Neither layer changes objective truth. This separation supports deliberate misinformation, dramatic irony, and later continuity review without treating prose inference as fact.

Threads may be Workspace-wide or scoped to a Work, Volume, or Arc. Explicit typed links connect them to Chapters, Scenes, Characters, Groups, world records, Saved Draws, and Draw Interpretations. Established records use status transitions rather than hard deletion. Resolution notes and story-time labels preserve payoff history, while author-controlled health and next-action fields remain planning signals rather than automated judgments.
