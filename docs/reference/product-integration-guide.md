# Product Integration Guide

Strange Novelty presents one creative workspace through a bounded set of primary destinations.

- **Workspace** is the command center and starting point.
- **Works** and **Scenes** hold structure, planning, prose, and immutable revisions.
- **Characters**, **Continuity**, and **Timeline** separate cast, narrative memory, and chronology.
- **World** groups Locations, Regions, factions and Groups, Codex entries, Items, and Creatures.
- **Decks** and **AI Studio** produce reviewable creative possibilities rather than automatic canon.
- **Library** keeps Research Sources, author Research Notes, Artwork, and Collections distinct from story truth.
- **Publishing** compiles explicit Scene Revisions into private reading copies and tracked exports.

Groups remain available from Character and World contexts rather than occupying duplicate primary navigation. The global Create page links into existing Workspace-scoped forms; it does not duplicate creation logic.

## Distinctions preserved

- Continuity answers what has been promised, hidden, learned, revealed, or resolved. Timeline answers when events occur and how chronology differs from reader order.
- Original Deck Card content, Draw configuration, author interpretation, and AI interpretation remain separate.
- AI output remains a suggestion until explicit application or conversion.
- Research evidence, author interpretation, and story application remain separate.
- Export status and publication status are independent. Manuscripts retain exact immutable Scene Revision provenance.

## Responsive behavior

The shared navigation becomes a compact two-column set of grouped destinations at narrow widths. Tables and complex module views retain their own horizontal overflow behavior, while long labels and record content wrap rather than forcing the application shell wider than the viewport.
